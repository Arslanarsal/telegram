========================================================
  TELEGRAM SENDER  —  how to use it
========================================================

WHAT IT DOES

  You type your message ONCE.
  You press one button.
  Every person on your list gets it as a normal private
  message from you.

  Nobody sees anybody else. There is no group. There is
  no channel. To them it looks exactly like you wrote to
  them personally, because you did.

  When somebody replies, their reply arrives in your
  normal Telegram, in your normal private chat with them.
  Only you and they can see it. Nothing to set up for
  that — it just works.

  No monthly fee. No yearly fee. Nothing to subscribe to.


--------------------------------------------------------
  STARTING IT
--------------------------------------------------------

  On Windows:   double-click   START WINDOWS.bat
  On Mac:       double-click   START MAC or LINUX.command

  The first time, it spends about a minute setting itself
  up. After that it opens in a few seconds.

  If Windows shows a blue "Windows protected your PC" box:
  click "More info", then "Run anyway". That appears
  because the file is new, not because anything is wrong.


--------------------------------------------------------
  FIRST TIME ONLY — connecting your Telegram
--------------------------------------------------------

  You do this once, ever. The window walks you through it.

  1. Click the blue "Open my.telegram.org" button.
  2. Log in there with your phone number. Telegram sends
     you a code in the app — type it in the website.
  3. Click "API development tools".
     (NOT "Delete account", which is just below it.)
  4. Fill it in exactly like this:
         App title:   mysender
         Short name:  mysender
         URL:         leave empty
         Platform:    Desktop
     Letters and numbers only, no spaces. Then Create.
     If it says "ERROR", reload the page - it often
     worked anyway and the values are there.
  5. The page then shows two things:
         api_id      (a number)
         api_hash    (a long code)
     Copy each one into the matching box in the window.
  6. Type your phone number with the country code,
     e.g.  +353871234567
  7. Click Connect.
  8. Telegram sends you a login code. Type it in the box
     that appears. If you use a Telegram password, it
     will ask for that too.

  Done. It remembers you from now on.


--------------------------------------------------------
  PROJECTS
--------------------------------------------------------

  At the top of the window there is a "Project" dropdown.

  A project is one job: its own message, its own list of
  people, and its own memory of who has already been sent
  to.

  So you can keep:

      Daily rates        -> 400 customers
      VIP customers      -> 30 people
      New enquiries      -> whoever came in this week

  Click New to make one, Rename to rename it, Delete to
  remove it. Switch between them with the dropdown and
  everything for that project comes back exactly as you
  left it.

  Sending in one project does NOT affect any other
  project.


--------------------------------------------------------
  ADDING EVERYONE FROM A GROUP
--------------------------------------------------------

  Click "Add everyone from a group..." under the list.

  It shows the groups your account is in. Pick one and
  everybody in it gets added to your list. Anyone already
  on the list is skipped, so you cannot get duplicates.

  Careful: people in a group who are not already your
  contacts count as NEW people. Only a small number of
  those are sent per day, on purpose — see below. Members
  of a group who have never spoken to you are exactly the
  people Telegram bans accounts for messaging.

  Some groups hide their member list unless you are an
  admin. Nothing we can do about that.


--------------------------------------------------------
  SENDING — the everyday routine
--------------------------------------------------------

  BOX 1 — YOUR MESSAGE

    Type your message on the left.

    Handy tricks (all optional):

      {name}
          becomes their first name.
          "Hi {name}," arrives as "Hi John,"

      {Hi|Hello|Hey}
          picks one at random for each person, so the
          messages are not all word-for-word identical.
          This is the single best thing you can do to
          keep your account safe.

      ---
          put three dashes on their own line to write a
          whole second version of the message. It will
          mix between them.

    Aim for two versions. The little label under the box
    turns green when you have enough.

  BOX 2 — WHO GETS IT

    One person per line, either:

        @their_username
        +353871234567

    You can add their name after a comma so {name} works:

        @john_doe, John
        +353871234567, Mary

    Or click "Load from a file…" and pick a file you
    already have.

  THEN

    Click "Check first (sends nothing)".
      It tells you how many will go out, how long it will
      take, and shows you exactly what one person will
      receive. Nothing is sent. Always do this.

    Click "START SENDING".
      It asks you to confirm, then starts.

    Leave the window open while it works.
    You can press STOP any time.


--------------------------------------------------------
  WHY IS IT SLOW?  (please read this bit)
--------------------------------------------------------

  It sends about one message every one to two minutes,
  and takes a rest every dozen or so. 100 messages takes
  around 4 hours.

  This is on purpose. It is the whole reason your account
  stays alive.

  Telegram watches for accounts firing out hundreds of
  identical messages in seconds, and it restricts them.
  By going at the speed of a person actually typing —
  with pauses, breaks, and slightly different wording —
  it looks like what it is: you, messaging your customers.

  Do not ask for it to be made faster. Faster is the one
  thing that gets the account banned.

  It also will not send at night. It waits until 9am.


--------------------------------------------------------
  HOW MANY CAN IT SEND?
--------------------------------------------------------

  There are TWO different groups, and they are treated
  completely differently. This matters.

  1. PEOPLE YOU ALREADY KNOW
     Your saved contacts, and anyone you already have a
     chat with.

     NO LIMIT. All of them. However many you select.

     The only thing that limits it is the clock, because
     each message is paced like a person typing. In a
     normal day it gets through a few hundred. If your
     list is bigger than that, it simply carries on the
     next day by itself.

  2. PEOPLE YOU HAVE NEVER MESSAGED
     Strangers. Group members who aren't your contacts.

     LIMITED to 15 a day by default.

     This is not us being cautious for no reason. This is
     the exact thing Telegram bans accounts for — their
     error is literally called "too many non-mutual
     users". Accounts that push this get restricted
     within days.

     You can change the number under "Speed & limits",
     but above about 40 a day you are gambling with the
     account.

  So: if you are messaging your own customers, you can
  send to all of them. If you are messaging strangers,
  it goes out in small daily batches whether you like it
  or not.


--------------------------------------------------------
  THE FIRST WEEK IS SMALLER ON PURPOSE
--------------------------------------------------------

  Day 1 allows 25 messages in total. Then 40, 60, 90,
  130, 180, 240 — and from day 8 there is no ceiling at
  all for people you know.

  Telegram trusts accounts that grow steadily and
  punishes sudden bursts. Anyone left over is not lost —
  open the window the next day and it carries straight on
  from where it stopped.


--------------------------------------------------------
  THINGS IT DOES FOR YOU AUTOMATICALLY
--------------------------------------------------------

  * Never sends to the same person twice in a campaign,
    even if you close it halfway through and come back.

  * Checks with Telegram (@SpamBot) before every single
    run, and refuses to send if your account is already
    in trouble.

  * Stops itself instantly if Telegram complains.

  * Waits patiently if Telegram asks it to slow down.

  * Sends to people you already know first, and only a
    few brand-new people each day. New people are the
    risky ones.

  * Keeps a full record in  sent_log.csv  — who, when,
    and whether it worked. Opens in Excel.


--------------------------------------------------------
  BUTTONS YOU MIGHT WONDER ABOUT
--------------------------------------------------------

  "Account health"
      Asks Telegram whether your account is fine. Use it
      any time you are unsure.

  "Send again to everyone..."
      Use this when you have a NEW message for the SAME
      people. It forgets who already received the last
      one, so everybody on that project's list can be
      messaged again.
      If you skip this, people who already got your last
      message will be skipped.

  "Speed & limits"
      Change the sending speed, how many new people per
      day, and the sending hours. The defaults are the
      safe ones — only change them if you understand the
      trade-off.


--------------------------------------------------------
  IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
--------------------------------------------------------

  "Cannot send today - account is limited"
      Telegram has restricted you. Nothing was sent.
      Open Telegram, message @SpamBot, press its buttons
      and appeal politely — say you were messaging your
      own customers. It is usually lifted in 1 to 3 days.
      Do not try again until it says you are clear.

  "TELEGRAM HAS FLAGGED THIS ACCOUNT FOR SPAM"
      It stopped itself to protect you. Do not send again
      today. Check @SpamBot tomorrow.

  "could not be found"
      That username or number is wrong, or that person
      has no Telegram. Fix the line and it will be picked
      up next run.

  "cannot receive messages from you"
      Their own privacy setting, or they blocked you.
      Nothing you can do.


--------------------------------------------------------
  THE ONE RULE
--------------------------------------------------------

  Only message people who expect to hear from you.

  This tool paces itself carefully, but nothing on earth
  can protect you from somebody pressing "Report Spam".
  A handful of those does more damage than thousands of
  well-paced messages. Send to your customers and
  contacts — not to strangers.
